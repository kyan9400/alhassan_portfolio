This file is a template. Copy it to `content/notes/<slug>.mdx`, replace the text, and set
`draft: false` when the post is ready. The file name becomes the URL: `/<lang>/notes/<slug>`, where `<lang>` is the post's `lang`.

## Frontmatter

Every note starts with a block of `key: value` lines:

- `title` and `description` appear in the list, the RSS feed and search results;
- `date` is `YYYY-MM-DD`;
- `lang` is `en`, `ru` or `ar` — Arabic posts are laid out right to left;
- `draft: true` keeps a post out of the list, the feed, the sitemap and production builds.

## Headings build the table of contents

Second- and third-level headings are listed at the top of the post and get their own anchors.

### A third-level heading

It is indented under its section in the table of contents.
