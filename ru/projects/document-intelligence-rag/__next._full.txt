Documents never leave the company network.

Search is hybrid, with no LLM generation: a custom inverted index over words and designations, plus FAISS semantic search on sentence-transformers embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2). Designations are matched by general rules: look-alike Latin and Cyrillic letters, spaces and hyphens do not break a match, while numbers and marks are never substituted. Scans are detected automatically and selected pages go through Tesseract + Poppler OCR. The backend is FastAPI, the interface is plain HTML/JS, and it runs as a systemd service on Ubuntu Server with an atomic index swap and rollback.

Code and mark queries went from 9–10 s to 1.2–1.7 s and phrase queries from 55–70 s to 2–4 s; after the move to the inverted index, p95 is 0.18 s on 55 control queries. Quality is held by 739 pytest tests and a 104-query control set with top-1/top-5 metrics. I built it with AI agents (Codex, Cursor, Claude Code): I gathered the requirements, wrote the agent briefs, reviewed the changes and checked them on the production index. The client accepted the work in September 2026.
